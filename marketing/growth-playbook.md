# Redline Society — Buyer-Conversion Playbook

The honest constraint: cold Meta broad traffic to an unknown $415K sweepstakes ~never buys
(0 of 145 clicks reached checkout). You can't tell Meta to "find buyers" until you HAVE
buyers. This playbook is the machine that manufactures the first buyers, then scales.

---

## STAGE 1 — Get engaged visitors (running now)
On the existing RS02 Traffic campaign:
- Performance goal: **Maximize landing page views** (set) — quality over cheap taps.
- Placements: **Manual → uncheck Audience Network + Messenger.** Keep FB + IG Feeds/Reels/Stories.
- Detailed targeting (add): **Porsche, Porsche 911, Sports cars, Luxury vehicles, Sweepstakes,
  Contests, Engaged Shoppers.** This is the closest proxy to "buyers" you can use with no
  conversion history — people who care about the car.
- Creative: the new $5/RS02 graphics + reels video.

This won't produce many direct sales — its job is to fill a **retargeting pool.**

## STAGE 2 — Retarget the hand-raisers (the real buyers)
Once Stage 1 has sent a few hundred site visitors, build these Custom Audiences
(Ads Manager → Audiences → Create → Custom Audience):
- **Website — all visitors, 30 days** (source: your pixel).
- **Website — InitiateCheckout, 30 days** (started checkout, didn't finish — HOTTEST).
- **Video — watched 50%+** of the reels ad, 30 days.
- **Engaged — IG/FB page + ad engagers, 30 days.**

New ad set targeting those audiences (combined), Traffic/LPV or — once it has volume —
a Sales objective. THIS is "serving the ad to people who actually buy": you're re-serving
people who already showed buying behavior. Retargeting converts 3–10x cold.

## STAGE 3 — Lookalikes (scale, only after ~15–30 purchases)
- Build a **Purchase Lookalike (1%, US)** off your purchaser pixel event or a customer list.
- THEN a Sales/Purchase-optimized cold campaign to that lookalike will deliver — Meta finally
  has a "buyer" pattern to match. Not before.

## EMAIL NURTURE — turn captured leads into buyers
The exit-popup now captures emails (EmailSubscriber). Load these into an ESP (Resend,
Mailchimp, ConvertKit) and send as an automation. This converts far better than any cold ad.

1. **Immediately** — Subject: "Your GT3 RS entry link (don't lose this)"
   Welcome + one-click link to the $5 entry. Reassure: real car, filmed draw, real RS01 winner.
2. **+1 day** — Subject: "Toby won a Lamborghini. You could be next."
   Lead with the RS01 winner (Toby Lei) proof + photo. Trust → the #1 blocker.
3. **+3 days** — Subject: "How the odds actually work (better than you think)"
   Explain entries/odds honestly; anchor the $5 entry vs $250 best-odds. Show the math.
4. **+5 days** — Subject: "$5 gets you in. Here's what you get."
   Value stack: instant poster + entries + shot at $415K / $300K cash. Single CTA.
5. **+7 days / boost deadline** — Subject: "The 200x boost ends [date] — after that your $ buys half"
   Real urgency (code-enforced boost drop). Last call before multipliers fall.

Rules: real claims only, one CTA per email, plain-text-feel, mobile-first, easy unsubscribe.

---

## THE BLUNT STRATEGIC NOTE
Car-giveaway winners (roadcourseclub etc.) don't live on cold Meta. They build a **warm
audience first**: an IG following, **influencer/creator shoutouts** (car pages, supercar
accounts), an email list, and retargeting. If you want volume buyers fast, one influencer
post to a car-enthusiast audience will out-convert months of cold broad ads. That's the
highest-leverage move that isn't a targeting checkbox.
